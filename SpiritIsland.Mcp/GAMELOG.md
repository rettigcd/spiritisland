# Game log: growth and Presence track choices

One section per game, so growth strategy can be compared across games. **Keep this up to date every game.** For each round record:

- **Growth:** which group was chosen (Reclaim = the Reclaim All / Gain Power Card / Gain 1 Energy group; Presence = the Place Presence group(s); Energy+Presence = Lightning's Gain 3 Energy + Place Presence(1); Major = Gain Power Card with a major draw).
- **Track:** where each Presence came from: **E** = energy track, **C** = card play track, **R** = a track slot taken indirectly (e.g. via Indomitable Claim). Include the value uncovered, e.g. `E2` = the "2 energy" slot.
- **Income / plays / cards played** at the start of the round (from `energyPerTurn`, `cardPlaysPerTurn`), and what the round achieved.

The question this log is for: should a spirit push the energy track and play few strong cards, push card plays and play many cheap ones, or split? Compare games before drawing conclusions. So far the sample is tiny and confounded by seed, spirit and card draws.

## What the data suggests so far (hypotheses, not conclusions)

- **River (games 5 and 6, same seed):** the P R P R P R... sequence (game 6) won in round 8. Game 5, which played Reclaim in 7 of 8 rounds and took a major, never found a win. So presence growth early, mostly on the card-play track up to 4 plays, with Reclaim All in between, looks better for River than Reclaim-heavy play. One seed, one game each; not proof the exact order is the best.

- The one win (game 3) took Presence growth in 3 of 5 rounds and used both tracks. The long Lightning loss (game 2) took Presence growth in only 2 of 10 rounds and ran at income 2 and 2 plays for most of the game. Both Lightning games had the same pattern in the early rounds, so what differed was how long Reclaim All stayed my default.
- In every game, being **card-play-limited** hurt more than being energy-limited (River's idle 10-12 Energy; Lightning's round 8-10 ravages needing 3-4 plays). I'd weight the card play track more heavily after income reaches about 2-3.
- Reclaim All every round gives a free card but never raises either track. It looks like a good default when hand cards are in the discard, and a poor default when I already have plenty of cards.
- Needs more data: try the opposite choice on the same spirit and seed (energy-heavy vs plays-heavy) and compare.
